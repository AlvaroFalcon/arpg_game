# The world: premise, tone and names **[DRAFT 2026-11-11]**

Issue #56 (tone and identity) and #57 (premise and goal), from the player feedback of
2026-11-09 (epic #53): the game did not tell the player where they are, why, or what
kind of fantasy it is. This doc is the reference every other piece of copy, art and
naming checks against. **Undecided:** the game's name, the world's name, the
antagonist's name (see "Names" at the end).

## The premise (owner, 2026-11-11)

An **isekai**: you died in your own world and woke up in this one, with a purpose.

- A powerful being (the **Antagonist**, name open) **distorted reality**. Worlds that
  should never touch are bleeding into each other and fusing: a crypt with a forge's
  heart in it, a temple drowned in someone else's sea.
- You were **reborn** here, bound to **the Mirror of Realities**, the one thing that can
  still reach those fused worlds and set them right.
- Each Mirror Shard opens one **Altered Reality**. Clearing it (killing what anchors the
  fusion, its boss) **stabilises** it, and the Mirror grows stronger, so it can reach
  deeper, more distorted realities (the tiers).
- **Dying inside a reality cracks the Mirror a little.** Crack it too often and it loses
  its hold on that reality: you are thrown back home and the shard is spent. This
  replaces "portals left" (issue #54).
- Long-term goal: stabilise enough realities to reach the source of the distortion and
  face the Antagonist. Short-term goal, every session: get stronger, go deeper.

In one line, for the menu or the store page:

> *You died. You woke up in a world where every world is breaking into every other.
> Only the Mirror can hold them apart, and it chose you.*

### Why an isekai fits this game

- **It explains the newcomer.** The player knows nothing, and neither does the
  character: the tutorial is literally your first hours in a new world.
- **It explains respawning.** You come back because the Mirror holds you, and every
  return costs it something: that is the attempts counter, now with meaning.
- **It explains the mix of themes.** Ash, bone and drowned realities, a magma golem in a
  crypt (the feedback's complaint) stop being a contradiction: they are pieces of
  different worlds fused together. The fusion *is* the setting.
- **It gives the classes a past without lore.** You chose who you were reborn as, and a
  line about your last life is enough for the player to build the rest (see below).

### Mapping onto what already exists

| In the game | In the fiction |
|---|---|
| Hideout / town | The **haven**: the one stable place, built around the Mirror. Safe, warm, alive. |
| Mirror of Realities | Your anchor. Shards are pieces of it, scattered by the distortion. |
| Mirror Shards, tiers | Higher tier = a reality more deeply distorted, further from stable. |
| Portals left on death | **Mirror stability**: cracks on the glass. Last crack = thrown home. |
| Reality boss | The **anchor** of the fusion: what holds two worlds stuck together. Kill it, the reality settles. |
| Exit portal after the boss | The reality is stable; the Mirror pulls you home. |
| The Threshold (tutorial) | Where you wake up after dying. The Mirror's first test, and how it finds you. |
| Survival Rift | A **wound**: a place where worlds collide non-stop and never settle. You don't stabilise it, you survive it. |
| Mirror jewels | Strengthening the Mirror itself, account-wide (it is *your* Mirror, whoever you are reborn as). |
| NPCs (Odran, Ysolde, Escaldón, the Artificer, Arehucaz) | Others who ended up in the haven: some reborn like you, some pulled in from fused worlds. Each can carry one line about where they came from. |

### The classes as "who you were reborn as" (issue #62)

One line about the last life, shown at character creation, separate from the mechanics.
First drafts, to pick from or rewrite:

- **Warrior.** *"You died standing between someone and the thing that killed you. You
  woke up still standing."*
- **Rogue.** *"You died running: late, as always, and too fast. Here, fast is enough."*
- **Thaumaturge.** *"You died because you had to know what would happen. You still do."*

## Tone

**Bright fantasy, with a wound in it.** Not grimdark, not cosy-only.

- **The haven is light**: warm gold, cream stone, greenery, lanterns, music you want to
  stay in. It is what you fight to protect, and it lets the art style (round, cute
  characters) live comfortably.
- **The Altered Realities are wrong**: each keeps its own world's palette (ash red,
  bone white, drowned teal) but is **cracked by the Mirror's light**: violet-silver
  seams, refracted light, mirrored geometry, things that don't belong. The deeper the
  tier, the more cracked and saturated.
- **The danger is real, not gory**: menace through scale, sound and light (a colossus
  glowing through the dark, chanting from the next room), not through blood.
- **Hope as the through-line**: every stabilised reality is a small win you can see
  (the Mirror brighter, a crack closed).

Three to five words to check things against: **luminous, fractured, wondrous,
dangerous, hopeful.**

### Visual motifs

- **Glass, silver and cracks**: the Mirror's language. Shards, frames, seams of light.
  It is the one element shared by every reality and the UI.
- **Reflections**: water, polished stone, a boss that is a reflection (Nereth already is).
- **Two worlds in one place**: the strongest image of the setting. Every reality should
  show at least one thing from "the other world" (a forge in a crypt, coral in a hall).

### Palette

| Place | Dominant | Accent |
|---|---|---|
| Haven (hideout) | warm gold, cream, soft green | lantern orange |
| Mirror / UI of the Mirror | silver, deep blue | **violet** (already the portal and shard colour) |
| Ashen realities | ember red, charcoal | violet cracks |
| Bone realities | bone white, moss | violet cracks |
| Drowned realities | teal, deep blue | violet cracks |
| Survival Rift | all of them at once, unstable | white flashes |

The violet already used for portals and the Mirror becomes **the colour of the Mirror's
power**: it should mean "the Mirror / stability" everywhere and nothing else.

### Sound and music

- Haven: warm, melodic, a small ensemble.
- Realities: the haven's theme, distorted (detuned, reversed fragments), plus the
  reality's own colour.
- Glass sounds as the Mirror's signature: a crack when you die, a clear ring when a
  reality stabilises.

## Names (brainstorm, undecided)

Naming rules, from what already exists (Escaldón, Arehucaz, Odran, Ysolde, Nereth):
names that sound like they come from several languages (the worlds are fused), easy to
say in Spanish and English, no apostrophes.

### Game title

Searched on 2026-11-11: no released game uses these names. Similar titles exist:
*Mirrorbound* (itch.io), *Mirror Forge*, *The Secret of Velmora*, *GlassGod*.

| Name | Idea | For | Against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Azogue** | Spanish for the quicksilver behind a mirror | Short, unique, Spanish flavour like Escaldón; "what makes a mirror reflect" | Obscure word in English; needs a subtitle |
| **Velmira** | *vel* (veil) + *mira* (to look) | Sounds like a fantasy world; can be both the world and the title | Close to "Velmora" (an indie title) |
| **Mirrorborn** | Born from the Mirror | Says isekai and Mirror in one word | Close to *Mirrorbound*; generic in English |
| **Otherglass** | The world behind the glass | Evocative, English, simple | Sounds more like a puzzle game |
| **Thousand Reflections** | Every world is one reflection | Says "many worlds" | Long, descriptive |

A common pattern: world name + subtitle, e.g. *Velmira: Shards of the Mirror*.

#### Second round, English only (2026-11-11)

Grouped by what the name says. "Free" means a search on 2026-11-11 found no game with
that name.

| Name | Says | Search |
|---|---|---|
| **Mirrorwake** | Waking up through the Mirror (the isekai) | Free |
| **Mirrorfall** | The Mirror breaking; the fall into other worlds | Free |
| **Worldseam** | The seams where worlds are stitched together | Free (only *World Seed*) |
| **Twiceborn** | Your second life | Free, but *Twice Reborn* (a vampire visual novel) exists |
| **Seamless** | A world without seams… and a pun | Generic word, hard to search for |
| **The Silvered Path** | The silver behind a mirror, the way through it | Not checked |
| **Glasswake** | Like Mirrorwake | **Taken**: an upcoming Steam game |
| **Silverfall** | Like Mirrorfall | **Taken**: an action RPG from 2007 |
| **Refraction** | Light bent through glass | **Taken** by several games |

Close competitor to keep in mind: *Fractured Worlds*, an upcoming dark-fantasy action RPG
"in the shattered remnants of a ruined reality". Avoid "fractured" and "shattered"
in the title.

Shortlist after this round: **Mirrorwake** (says the premise: you wake up through the
Mirror) and **Mirrorfall** (says the conflict: the Mirror breaking); **Worldseam** if the
title should be about the fusing worlds rather than the Mirror.

### The world

If the title is not the world's name: Velmira, Aelmir, Ilvaren, Myrrhen.

### The Antagonist

Directions:
- **A name that sounds like breaking**: Oruvan the Unmaker, Kaivor, Nhalazar.
- **A title instead of a name**: "the Shatterer", "the Unwoven".
- **A twist kept for later**: the Antagonist was the Mirror's **previous chosen one**, who
  broke reality trying to bring their own world back. Fits the isekai premise and the
  reflections motif (Nereth, the Drowned Reflection, could be the first hint).

## Open questions

- The names (title, world, Antagonist).
- Do we ever show the world the character came from (one image in the intro), or only
  hint at it?
- Does stabilising a reality leave a visible trace in the haven (a crack mended on the
  Mirror, a new decoration)? It would make progress visible.
- How much the NPCs say about their own origin.
