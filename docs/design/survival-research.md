# Survival Rift — research on Vampire Survivors, Megabonk and Hell Clock

Status: **research, nothing decided** (2026-11-07). Owner request (translated): *"Research
similar games — Vampire Survivors, Megabonk, Hell Clock — how they move between levels,
how they scale, how they hand out rewards and what progression they give the player in
survival mode. We want to improve this mode, so take ideas from the games that work.
Also look for feedback online: people proposing improvements to those games that we
could adopt."*

How to read it: one section per game (what it does, in the four areas the owner named),
then what players complain about and ask for, then a side-by-side with our Rift
([run.md](run.md), [progression.md](progression.md)) and a ranked list of proposals.
Numbers come from community wikis and guides (sources at the end). Where they
disagree, or a figure came from one fan site only, the text says so. Reddit could not be
read from this session, so player feedback comes from Steam discussions, store-review
summaries, press reviews and comment threads.

## 1. Vampire Survivors (the genre's template)

**Structure.** One stage, 30 minutes. A Reaper arrives at 30:00 and ends the run (one
more every minute after that). Bosses on a schedule drop the chests. Late unlocks add
modes on top of the same stages: **Hyper** (everything faster, plus a gold/luck bonus,
unlocked by killing the 25:00 boss), **Inverse** (tougher enemies, more gold), **Endless**
(no Reaper: the stage loops and keeps scaling).

**Between levels (XP and the level-up).** The XP needed rises by a flat step per level:
5 to reach level 2, then +10 per level up to 20, +13 up to 40 and +16 after that. Levels 20
and 40 also add a lump (+600 and +2,400 XP) and grant +100 % growth until the next level,
which smooths the bump. Each level-up pauses and offers **3 cards, sometimes 4 (Luck)**,
drawn from weapons and passive items, at most **6 of each**. Owned items have a better
chance to show up on even levels. **Reroll, Skip and Banish** are limited charges bought
with the meta currency. When everything is maxed, a level-up gives gold or a healing
chicken, or with **Limit Break** extra levels for maxed weapons. A short invulnerability
follows every pick.

**Scaling.** Mostly the stage's timed wave list (fixed per stage), plus the player's own
**Curse** stat, which raises enemy speed, life, spawn rate and count. Curse is opt-in
difficulty that players stack for faster runs.

**Rewards in a run.** **Chests are the second progression track.** Only bosses drop them.
A chest holds 1, 3 or 5 upgrades: the 5 is checked first, then the 3, then the 1, each
chance multiplied by Luck (example boss: 3 % / 10 % / 50 %). Each upgrade levels a random
owned item, and the chest pays 100–1,000 gold by size. **Evolutions** arrive the same way:
a weapon at max level (8) plus its paired passive turns into its evolved form when the
next eligible chest opens. Those are silver chests, mostly after 10:00. **Arcana** (pick 1
of 4–6 run-wide rule changers, up to 3) come from chests at 0:00, 11:00 and 21:00.

**Meta progression.** Gold buys **PowerUps** (permanent small stats: might, armour,
growth, luck, curse, and the reroll/skip/banish charges), and they can be refunded at any
time. Most content (characters, weapons, stages, Arcana, modes) unlocks by achievements:
"evolve X", "survive 20 min with Y", "find the secret".

**What makes it work.** Two clocks per run: level-ups (small, frequent choices) and
chests (rare, big, unseen in advance). Evolutions turn the build into a puzzle with a
payoff after 10:00. The opt-in Curse dial gives the late game a challenge without new
content.

## 2. Megabonk (3D, built around exploration)

**Structure.** A run is **1–3 stages** depending on the chosen **tier** (tier 1 = one
stage, 2 = two, 3 = three). A stage is about **10 minutes** on a large open map: a
miniboss at 3:00 and 8:00, swarms at 4:00 and 7:00. The stage boss is behind a **Boss
Portal** hidden somewhere on the map (red wind points to it), which the player can
activate early, before 10:00. Killing the boss opens the portal to the next stage, on
tiers 2 and 3. At 10:00 **Final Swarm** starts: endless overtime that pays more and more
silver, up to an **8× multiplier** (16× before a patch cut it). Surviving 1, 2 and 6
minutes of it unlocks characters. Clearing a tier unlocks the next tier and challenge
modifiers for the one cleared.

**Between levels.** The level-up picks from **weapons and tomes** (stat books). Each
upgrade rolls a **rarity**, and Luck pushes it up. Stacks have no cap: guides talk of
level 80–150 at default difficulty and 1,000+ with heavy difficulty stacking. Reroll,
Skip and Banish exist, and runs are planned around **legendary** upgrades ("2–3
legendaries before the stage 3 boss"). The XP Tome is +7 % XP per stack after a nerf from
9 %.

**Scaling.** Difficulty is a **visible stat** that rises with time and with the player's
choices: each **Greed Shrine** is +5 % difficulty for more XP and gold, and some items add
+8 % or +10 %. Higher difficulty brings more and faster enemies that hit harder, and also
**more XP and coins**. The same knob is the challenge and the reward.

**Rewards in a run.** The map is full of **interactables**, and the run is a route
through them:
- **paid chests** (price rises with each purchase; the community says build Luck before
  buying, 15–30 %+);
- **free chests** from bosses and from elites summoned by **Challenge/Moai shrines**;
- **Charge shrines** (stand in the circle until it fills for a buff or healing);
- the **Succ/Magnet shrine** (pulls all XP: players loop the map first, then trigger it
  for several levels at once);
- **microwaves** (duplicate an item, or trade it for one of your choice at the same
  rarity).

**Meta progression.** **Silver** (from survival time, kills and objectives, failed runs
included) unlocks weapons, items, tomes and characters. Nothing permanent changes stats:
a reviewer compares it unfavourably with Halls of Torment on exactly that point. A long
**quest/challenge list** ("kill 1K skeletons", "clear Forest tier 2 with X") drives
unlocks. The Toggler lets players remove unlocked items from the pool, because "unlocking
too many dilutes the pool".

**What makes it work.** The run is a **route**: find the portal, hit shrines, buy chests,
decide when to call the boss. The player **chooses when to raise difficulty** and is paid
for it immediately. Overtime has a hard cap and milestone unlocks, so "how long do I stay"
is a real decision.

## 3. Hell Clock (ARPG-roguelite with a clock)

**Structure.** A run descends floors. Each act has 3 bosses (4 acts with the DLC), and a
floor-7 merchant boss unlocks the meta tree. A **countdown** (7:00 at the start) ends the
run at zero, and **bosses and minibosses add time**. Relaxed Mode turns the clock off.
Endgame modes: **Endless Nightmares** (3 random areas of 7 floors, a survival wave on
floor 3 and a boss on floor 7, **16 tiers** in steps of four World Tiers),
**Abyss/Oblivion** (NG+ with resistance penalties) and **Ascension** (limited runs,
everything resets, difficulty points called Penances).

**Between levels.** A level-up offers **1 of 3 skill blessings (Proficiencies)** with
**rarity caps per skill**: 4 Common, 3 Magic, 2 Rare and 1 Epic, plus rare Unique
Proficiencies. Other blessings are **bought with run gold at shrines**: Foundation
blessings follow a fixed path and apply to every skill; Future blessings offer relics or
gear and can be rerolled. Bosses offer **free Legacy blessings (Unique Relics)**.
**Rerolls cost gold, and the price rises for the rest of the run.**

**Scaling.** Enemy life rises with depth and act. Endless tiers come with **Shards of
Fear** (negative modifiers) and **Shards of Hope** (positive ones), chosen at the start of
each area.

**Rewards and meta.** **Relics** are kept between runs, in a limited grid, and upgraded
with Soul Stones (250 → 6,000 per level). **Trinkets** are run-only: one per stat, and a
better roll replaces the worse. The **Great Bell** tree gets its points from **total Soul
Stones ever collected**, so nothing is spent and nothing is lost. Its nodes include more
XP (more blessings per run), timer +120 s, a revive per run (Second Wind), Red Portals
that skip cleared floors for a temporary XP/gold bonus, and higher skill-level caps.

**What makes it work.** **Gold has two jobs in a run**: rerolls, and shrine blessings
that are a second path to power besides levels. The **clock rewards killing**, which makes
aggression the right play. The meta tree **cannot be "spent wrong"**.

## 4. What players complain about, and what they ask for

Grouped by theme. Only the ones that apply to a mode like ours are kept.

| Theme | Where | What players say | What we could take |
|---|---|---|---|
| **"I just stand and wait for the level-up"** | Vampire Survivors (Steam, DLC adventures) | Once a build is strong there is nothing to do; asks for enemies that adapt to the player's power. Replies: "it is very easy to become strong". | Our auto-cast has the same risk. Objectives that make the player move (shrines, chests to reach, a portal to find), and a difficulty that follows the player's choices rather than only the clock. |
| **Too easy once everything is unlocked** | VS (Steam) | Asks for new weapons and characters. The community answer is self-imposed handicaps (banish everything, keep weapons at level 1). | Built-in opt-in difficulty: Rift tiers and modifiers (Curse, Greed, Shards of Fear) that pay better. |
| **Sudden difficulty spikes** | Megabonk (GamingOnLinux comments), Hell Clock (Steam: "difficulty ALL OVER THE PLACE", Abyss, some bosses) | Spikes feel unfair, especially early in a run when there are few choices. | Keep the danger curve smooth (run.md -> "The danger level") and announce every step on the HUD. Never spike while the build has no answer yet. |
| **Bosses as pure DPS checks** | Hell Clock (Steam: the Curse that stacks damage taken until the boss dies) | Penalises tanky builds; damage over time and ground effects applied it several times a tick. | Don't add enrage-style timers that only damage can beat. Our bosses should test movement. |
| **Too much RNG** | Megabonk (Steam review, 2,200 h) | Late runs depend too much on luck; one mechanic is underused, which limits builds. | Banish/skip, bad-luck protection on key cards, and guaranteed picks at milestones (we already guarantee a skill card). |
| **Weak loot, only the best tier matters** | Hell Clock (Steam: "only unique relics are worth using", Nightmare loot poor) | Most drops are scrap; asks for fewer but better uniques and Alt to see roll ranges. | Our item cards should keep a clear "this is better" signal. Rare guarantees by tier already go this way. |
| **Meta grind with tiny steps** | Survivors-likes in general (Crafty Survivors: +2 % upgrades; "meta progression hides a weak core loop") | Small permanent percentages feel like nothing and blur whether you played well. | Prefer unlocks (new cards, start picks, modes) over +1 % stats. We already have the persistent character for stats. |
| **Item carry-over too slow** | Halls of Torment (Steam: one item saved per run) | Progress stalls. | We keep 1–3 items by tier; keep it at least that generous. |
| **Repetitive maps** | Hell Clock (reviews, Steam; the developer agreed and promised more randomisation) | Layouts become predictable after a few runs. | We have one arena. A second biome is already an open question in run.md. |
| **"Replace the final boss with a massive final wave"**, **"optional infinite mode"** | Megabonk (community fix list, GamingOnLinux) | Wants the run to end on a horde, and to keep going if you want. | We already have the endless phase and the cash-out offer. A final horde before the Colossus is cheap to try. |
| **No-timer option praised** | Hell Clock (Steam Deck HQ review) | Relaxed Mode for players who find the clock stressful. | Not needed: our clock does not end the run. |

## 5. Our Rift next to them

| | Vampire Survivors | Megabonk | Hell Clock | **Our Rift today** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Run length | 30 min, Reaper | 10 min × 1–3 stages + overtime | 7 min clock, bosses add time | 7:30 to the Colossus, then endless with a cash-out offer |
| Level-up | 3–4 cards (Luck), reroll/skip/banish | rarity-rolled weapons and tomes, reroll/skip/banish | 1 of 3 blessings, rarity caps per skill | 3 cards (skill / power-up / support), lesser and great keystones every 5/10, 2 rerolls per run |
| Second power track | **chests** (1/3/5, evolutions, Arcana) | **interactables** (chests, shrines, microwaves) | **shrines bought with gold**, boss relics | none: power-ups on the floor (Cataclysm, Lodestone) are tactical, not build |
| Difficulty dial | Curse stat, Hyper, Inverse | visible Difficulty, Greed shrines, tiers 1–3 | tiers, Shards of Fear and Hope, Ascension | the danger level follows the clock only; no tiers (open question) |
| Run gold | meta currency | chests, rerolls | shrines, rerolls | paid out at the end only |
| Meta | refundable PowerUps, achievement unlocks | silver unlocks, quests, Toggler | Great Bell from total Soul Stones, relics | the persistent character (gear, tree, ascendancy) plus gold and orbs |
| Endless | Endless mode (no Reaper) | Final Swarm, silver ×8 cap, milestone unlocks | Endless Nightmares, 16 tiers | endless phase with reward tiers I–X (rare guarantees, Ascendant orbs) |

What we already do well: the ARPG layer none of them has to this degree (gear, orbs and
the passive tree carry over), a visible reward ladder in the endless phase, cash-out as a
choice, and keystones as Arcana-like rule changers.

What we lack, compared with all three:
1. A **second power track inside the run**: the chests, shrines and microwaves of the
   others.
2. **Opt-in risk for reward**: Curse, Greed and Shards of Fear.
3. **Something to do besides kiting**: a route, objectives. This matters even more
   because we auto-cast.
4. A **build "payoff" moment**: evolutions, legendaries, unique relics.
5. **Pick control**: skip and banish, and luck as a stat.
6. **Long-term goals across Rift runs**: challenges and unlocks that are about the Rift,
   not only gear.

## 6. Proposals, ranked

Effort is a rough guess against the current code. Each one would get its own design pass
before anything is built.

| # | Proposal | Inspired by | Why | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Rift chests** from elites and bosses: 1, 3 or 5 upgrades (levels of owned skills or power-ups, applied with no pause and listed on screen), the size rolled with a new **Luck** stat. Same queue and pickup code as loot. | VS chests, Megabonk free chests | A second, surprising power track. Fills the gap between level-ups in the endless phase. | M |
| 2 | **Skill evolutions**: a skill at level 6 plus a named power-up or support becomes an evolved form (Fireball + Volley → "Fire Storm"), delivered by the next boss chest after 5:00. | VS evolutions, Hell Clock unique relics | The build "payoff" we lack; gives cards a goal. Reuses skill trees (a hidden node set). | L |
| 3 | **Shrines in the arena**: *Greed* (+5 % danger for the rest of the run, +XP and gold), *Challenge* (summons an elite, which drops a chest), *Charge* (stand in the circle for 3 s to pick 1 of 3 run stats), *Magnet* (pulls every gem). 2–4 per run at random places, shown on the minimap. | Megabonk shrines, Hell Clock shrines | Gives auto-cast players a reason to move and a route to plan. Risk for reward on the player's terms. | M |
| 4 | **Rift tiers and modifiers**: beating the Colossus unlocks tier 2, and so on (denser, tougher, better item level and rarity). Plus optional **modifier cards before the run**: Fear (harder, +reward) and Hope (a small buff). | Megabonk tiers, Hell Clock Endless + Shards, HoT Agony | Answers run.md's open question; "too easy once unlocked" is the most common complaint about VS. All the director's numbers are already in one place. | M |
| 5 | **Skip and Banish** next to the 2 rerolls (skip gives back 30 % of the level's XP; banish removes a card from the pool for the run). Rerolls bought with **run gold** at a rising price, alongside the free ones. | VS, Megabonk, Hell Clock | Less RNG frustration; gives run gold a use before the results screen. | S |
| 6 | **Rift challenges** in the quest log (`QuestBook`): "survive 5 min of the endless phase", "evolve a skill", "win with only melee", "clear tier 3 with 3 Fear cards". Rewards: new start-pick skills, new keystones in the pool, cosmetics, or a stash tab. No % stats. | Megabonk quests, VS achievements, HoT quests | Long-term goals that are about the Rift. The quest system exists already. | S–M |
| 7 | **A final horde before the Colossus** (a 30 s ring wave at 6:30), and **milestones in the endless phase** that unlock things (5, 10 and 20 min → a challenge, a card in the pool). | Megabonk community ("massive final wave"), Megabonk Final Swarm unlocks | A climax, and reasons to stay in the endless phase beyond loot. | S |
| 8 | **Luck as a stat** (cards: a 4th level-up card, card rarity, chest size). | VS, Megabonk | Hooks proposals 1, 3 and 5 together. | S, with proposal 1 |
| 9 | **A second arena/biome** with its own enemies and boss, chosen in the Rift portal. | Megabonk maps, VS stages; Hell Clock repetition complaint | Variety. The biggest content cost on this list. | L |

**Suggested first slice:** proposals 1 + 8 + 5 (chests with luck, skip/banish, gold
rerolls), then 3 (shrines), then 4 (tiers). Each step can be measured with the autoplay
bot (`scripts/autoplay.sh`): time to kill, levels at 7:30, deaths.

Rejected or not recommended:
- **A run-ending timer** like Hell Clock's: our endless phase and cash-out already set
  the run's length.
- **Refundable permanent stat PowerUps** like VS's: the persistent character already
  plays that role, and players call tiny meta stats meaningless.
- **Paid chests with a rising price** like Megabonk's, as the main chest source: on an
  open arena with auto-cast, buying would be a pause-and-shop step. Free chests from
  elites and bosses fit better. Gold can go to rerolls instead (proposal 5).
- **Enemies that scale with the player's DPS** (a VS player's request): it makes upgrades
  feel pointless. Opt-in difficulty (proposals 3 and 4) answers the same complaint.

## Open questions

- Do evolutions use the run skill's level 6 cap, or a separate "mastery" track?
- Should chests pause the game (VS: a short animation) or apply silently with a feed line?
  Co-op makes pausing costly.
- Do shrines and chests split per player or go to the party in co-op? (multiplayer.md
  rules for level-ups suggest: party-wide.)
- Do Rift tiers share the Mirror's tier numbers (one ladder for the endgame) or have
  their own?
